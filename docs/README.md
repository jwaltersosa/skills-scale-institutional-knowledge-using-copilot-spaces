# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

## Overview

This documentation hub provides the core project management guidance used by OctoAcme to plan, execute, deliver, and improve cross-functional work. The approach is centered on clear ownership, customer value, iterative delivery, and evidence-driven decision-making. The goal is to help teams move from a rough idea to a well-managed outcome with consistent communication, measurable progress, and a repeatable process for learning and improvement.

## Project Management Process Summary

OctoAcme’s process begins with project initiation, where teams validate the business need, identify stakeholders, define success criteria, and create a lightweight one-pager for the initiative. Once stakeholders align and a go/no-go decision is made, the team moves into planning to scope the work, estimate effort, create a backlog, identify dependencies, and agree on milestones and the definition of done. During execution, the team manages day-to-day delivery through standups, status tracking, risk monitoring, and transparent communication. Release and deployment are standardized to reduce operational risk through pre-release checks, smoke testing, rollback planning, and stakeholder communication. Finally, each project closes with a retrospective to capture lessons learned, document follow-up actions, and drive continuous improvement.

## Roles and Responsibilities

OctoAcme uses clear functional roles to support accountability and collaboration across the project lifecycle. Product managers define the customer problem and outcomes; project managers coordinate schedules, risks, and communication; developers build and validate the solution; QA/testing verifies quality and acceptance criteria; and stakeholders provide input, priorities, and approvals. These roles create a shared operating model that helps onboarding, decision-making, and delivery across teams.

## Communication and Quality Practices

OctoAcme emphasizes regular communication and disciplined quality practices. The team uses standups, weekly status reviews, milestone check-ins, and escalation paths for blockers and dependencies to keep work visible and aligned. Quality is treated as an ongoing practice, including unit, integration, and end-to-end testing where appropriate, CI validation, security scanning, and manual QA for feature acceptance. By combining clear communication rhythms with defined quality gates and release checks, OctoAcme helps teams reduce uncertainty and deliver reliable outcomes.

## Core Principles

- Customer-first: prioritize customer value and usability
- Iterative delivery: deliver small, testable increments
- Clear ownership: define who is accountable for outcomes and coordination
- Data-informed decisions: use metrics and evidence to guide decisions
- Psychological safety: encourage feedback, learning, and continuous improvement

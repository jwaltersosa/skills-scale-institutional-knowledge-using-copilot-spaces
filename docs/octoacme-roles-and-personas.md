# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality assurance strategy, test planning, and validation for features before release. They ensure that acceptance criteria are met and that products meet quality standards before deployment.

### Responsibilities
- Define comprehensive test plans and QA strategies aligned with project scope
- Coordinate testing efforts across the team and identify test coverage gaps
- Validate features against acceptance criteria and quality standards
- Identify quality gaps and work with developers on testability improvements
- Track quality metrics and report on test results and defects
- Participate in design reviews to identify testability concerns early

### Goals
- Ensure product quality and reliability
- Reduce production defects and rework
- Enable confident, low-risk releases
- Maintain fast feedback loops for developers

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and test reports
- QA sign-offs on features and releases
- Quality metrics and trend analysis
- Collaboration with developers on testability and test automation
- Bug reports and severity assessments

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Reviews code for testability, proposes test automation strategies, collaborates on test coverage
- **With Product Managers**: Clarifies acceptance criteria to ensure they are testable, validates that features meet requirements
- **With Project Managers**: Reports on quality status, escalates blockers related to quality, participates in release planning

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove team impediments, and promote continuous improvement practices. They coach the team on agile methodologies and help maintain a sustainable, high-performing delivery rhythm.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate standups, retrospectives, sprint planning, and sprint reviews
- Coach team members on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Identify and escalate blockers and team impediments
- Track sprint health and velocity trends
- Foster psychological safety and encourage open communication
- Remove process bottlenecks and organizational impediments

### Goals
- Keep the team focused and moving toward sprint goals
- Maintain sustainable velocity and prevent burnout
- Foster psychological safety and a learning culture
- Reduce time spent in meetings while improving their effectiveness

### Typical Communication
- Sprint ceremonies (standups, planning, retrospectives, reviews)
- Impediment tracking and escalation
- Retrospective action items and coaching conversations
- Metrics and team health dashboards

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Removes blockers, facilitates technical discussions, coaches on estimation and sprint commitments
- **With Product Managers**: Ensures backlog is refined, facilitates prioritization discussions, maintains stakeholder communication
- **With Project Managers**: Collaborates on timeline and resource management, escalates critical blockers, provides team capacity insights

---

## Technical Architect / Technical Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Architects provide technical direction, evaluate architectural decisions, and ensure technical feasibility. They guide the team on technology choices, design patterns, and technical best practices while maintaining system reliability and scalability.

### Responsibilities
- Design technical solutions and architecture for new features and initiatives
- Review system design and provide technical guidance to the development team
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Guide technology choices and evaluate trade-offs
- Maintain architecture decision records (ADRs) and technical documentation
- Mentor developers on design patterns and best practices
- Collaborate on technical strategy and long-term roadmap planning

### Goals
- Maintain system reliability, scalability, and performance
- Reduce technical debt and complex, hard-to-maintain code
- Enable team autonomy while providing technical guardrails
- Accelerate feature delivery through reusable components and patterns

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture decision records (ADRs)
- Technical documentation and design specifications
- Code review feedback on architectural patterns
- Risk assessments and technical feasibility studies
- Mentoring and technical discussions with the team

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Provides design guidance, reviews architecture decisions, mentors on technical best practices
- **With Product Managers**: Advises on technical feasibility of features, communicates technical trade-offs and constraints
- **With Project Managers**: Identifies technical dependencies and risks, estimates technical complexity, supports planning

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge the gap between business stakeholders and technical teams by clarifying requirements and gathering stakeholder needs. They ensure that solutions align with business objectives and that acceptance criteria clearly define expected behavior.

### Responsibilities
- Gather requirements from stakeholders and business teams
- Document user stories and acceptance criteria with clarity and detail
- Validate business logic and functional requirements
- Facilitate requirements review sessions with stakeholders and team members
- Identify gaps or ambiguities in requirements and propose resolutions
- Trace requirements through the development lifecycle to ensure completeness

### Goals
- Ensure solutions accurately address business needs and problems
- Reduce rework caused by misunderstood or incomplete requirements
- Improve stakeholder satisfaction and product adoption
- Accelerate delivery by preventing scope creep and requirement changes

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documentation and user stories
- User story creation and refinement sessions
- Stakeholder interviews and clarification notes
- Acceptance criteria and definition of done validation
- Requirements traceability and change impact analysis

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Clarifies acceptance criteria during development, answers questions about business intent
- **With Product Managers**: Collaborates on backlog prioritization, gathers stakeholder input, validates feature specifications
- **With Project Managers**: Provides requirements documentation, identifies scope risks, supports change management

---

## Stakeholder / Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Executive Sponsors provide business context, approve project strategy and budget, and ensure alignment with organizational goals. They remove organizational blockers and communicate project value to leadership and the broader organization.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and strategic outcomes for the project
- Approve project scope, budget, and resource allocation
- Resolve escalations and organizational blockers that team cannot address
- Communicate project value and progress to leadership and the organization
- Ensure alignment with organizational strategy and priorities
- Make go/no-go decisions at key milestones

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers measurable business value
- Manage organizational risk and resource allocation
- Maintain strategic alignment with organizational goals
- Enable team success by removing organizational barriers

### Typical Communication
- Status updates and milestone approvals
- Executive dashboards and business impact metrics
- Escalation resolution and decisions
- Stakeholder briefings and announcement of releases
- Strategic alignment and priority discussions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Project Managers**: Approves scope and timeline, resolves escalations, provides governance and oversight
- **With Product Managers**: Aligns on business objectives and success metrics, prioritizes against other initiatives
- **With Development Team**: Communicates business context and value, removes organizational impediments
